Our Story

From Panyu to the World

Rooted in the Jewelry Capital

Panyu District in Guangzhou is where over 90% of Hong Kong's jewelry exports are processed. This is the global epicenter of jewelry manufacturing — and we are right at the heart of it.

Our workshop sits among hundreds of skilled artisans who have been perfecting their craft for generations. When you work with us, you're not just getting a supplier — you're tapping into an entire ecosystem of jewelry expertise.

We specialize in 925 sterling silver jewelry — minimalist, timeless pieces that combine traditional craftsmanship with modern design. Every piece is nickel-free, hypoallergenic, and stamped for authenticity.
