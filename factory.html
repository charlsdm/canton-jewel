Factory Tour

Inside Our Jewelry Factory in Panyu, Guangzhou

Five stages of in-house production — see how your 925 silver jewelry is made, from CAD to finished piece.

Step 1

Prototyping — 3D Printing & Hand-Carved Wax

Every piece starts with precision. We combine 3D printing with hand-carved wax modeling to turn your design — from a CAD file or a simple sketch — into a flawless wax prototype, ready for casting. Your idea becomes a wearable piece in days, not weeks.

Step 2

Casting — Lost-Wax Casting in 925 Sterling Silver

Using the time-tested lost-wax casting method, we transform wax patterns into solid 925 sterling silver pieces with sharp detail retention. From delicate chains to bold statement rings, every cast is inspected before moving to the next stage.

Step 3

Stone Setting — Micro-Pavé & Prong Setting

Our setters specialize in micro-pavé and prong setting, placing each cubic zirconia or moissanite by hand under magnification. One stone at a time — this is where our decade of experience shows.

Step 4

Polishing & Finishing — Mirror Shine by Hand

After setting, every piece is hand-polished and finished to a mirror shine, with edges refined and settings checked for security. We treat finishing as a craft, not an afterthought.

Step 5

Quality Control — Inspected Before It Ships

Every order passes through our QC line in a clean, controlled environment. From material to final polish, we inspect for consistency, stone security, and finish quality — so what you receive matches what you approved.

Want a closer look? We offer live video tours and can walk you through any stage of production on a call.
