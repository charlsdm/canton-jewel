Logistics & Payment

Shipping & Payment Terms

Everything you need to know before placing an order

📦 Shipping

DHL Express

3–7 business days worldwide. Full tracking, signature on delivery. Our default courier.

FedEx

3–7 business days. Good alternative for US & Middle East destinations.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

Available for EU countries. We handle all customs clearance, duties, and VAT — you receive the package with zero paperwork. Ask for a DDP quote.

Freight Forwarder

You can use your own freight forwarder. We'll deliver to their warehouse in Guangzhou at no extra charge.

⚠️ Countries We Cannot Ship To

Due to sanctions and logistics restrictions, we currently cannot ship to: Iran, Russia, Yemen, Iraq, Afghanistan, North Korea, Syria, Sudan, and Crimea region. If you're unsure, contact us first.

💳 Payment

T/T Bank Transfer

For orders above $2,000. 30–50% deposit to start production, balance before shipping. Our company bank account details provided on invoice.

Wise

For orders $500–$2,000. Fast, low fees, no chargeback risk. USD, EUR, GBP accepted.

PayPal

For sample fees and small orders under $500. Friends & Family preferred. For larger orders we recommend Wise or T/T.

Western Union

Available for sample fees and small orders. Instant transfer, no chargeback.

📋 MOQ & Order Process

Minimum Order Quantity

30 pieces per style for most items. Some designs start at 20 pieces. Mixed-style orders accepted — contact us to discuss.

Custom Products MOQ

Custom designs (OEM/ODM): 30 pieces minimum. Custom packaging boxes: 300 pieces MOQ (standard boxes have no minimum).

Sample Orders

Samples: $15–30 per piece + shipping. 7-day turnaround. Sample fees fully deductible from your bulk order.

Production Timeline

Standard orders: 15–20 business days after deposit confirmation. Rush orders available — ask for availability.
